## 2. Frequency Clusters Identified

### Cluster A: 103–106 Hz (A♭2 band)
- Varanasi Surya Temple: 103.83 Hz (exact)
- Brahmapuri, Rajasthan: 103.5–105.4 Hz
- **Two sites, two regions of India, same frequency band**

### Cluster B: 110 Hz (A2)
- Varanasi "Ohm" Temple: 110.0 Hz (exact)
- West Kennet Long Barrow, UK: 110 Hz (published)
- Paleolithic caves, SW Europe: ~110 Hz (published)
- King's Chamber 4th harmonic fundamental: ~109.5 Hz
- **Cross-cultural, cross-millennial convergence on 110 Hz**

### Cluster C: 117–120 Hz (B♭2 band)
- King's Chamber sarcophagus: 114–122 Hz peak (published), ~120 Hz (Drew)
- Humayun's Tomb: ~120 Hz (B♭2 +45c)
- Gur-e-Amir back dome: ~119.7 Hz (B2 -41.8c)
- **Egypt, Delhi, Samarkand — three sites spanning 4000 years, same frequency**
- **Gur-e-Amir is the direct architectural ancestor of Humayun's Tomb**

### Cluster D: ~93 Hz (G♭2)
- Khiva dome (North Star): ~93.0 Hz (G♭2 +7c)
- **Outlier — possibly different dome geometry or material**

### Cluster E: ~233 Hz (B♭3) — First Overtone
- Gur-e-Amir N pole: 233.1 Hz (B♭3 -1.1c, near exact)
- Gur-e-Amir N pole: 227.5 Hz (B♭3 -41.1c, flat)
- **Approximately 2× the Cluster C fundamental (~120 Hz) — octave relationship**

## 6. Key Research Findings

### The Architectural Lineage
Gur-e-Amir (Samarkand, 1404) → Humayun's Tomb (Delhi, 1572). Same dome form, same octagonal plan, same cenotaph arrangement. Architect Mirak Mirza Ghiyas from Herat (Timurid territory). **Both buildings channel solstice/equinox light onto the tomb through the Qibla wall.** Both resonate at ~120 Hz.

### The Qibla–Solstice Coincidence
At Samarkand's latitude, the Qibla direction (240°) nearly coincides with the winter solstice sunset (239°). Builders got both functions from one alignment. At Delhi's latitude, the Qibla (267°) nearly coincides with equinox sunset (270°). **The builders exploited latitude-specific coincidences between religious and astronomical vectors.**

### The Solar Substrate
Islamic prayer times are defined by shadow measurements (sun altitude), not azimuth. The muwaqqit (mosque timekeeper) was a professional astronomer. The mosque architecture IS a sundial. Yet the Islamic calendar deliberately rejects solar cycles (pure lunar, no intercalation). **The buildings worship the sun while the theology does not.**

### The City as Calendar (Khiva hypothesis)
Differently-oriented buildings within a single city collectively span the solar year. No single building is a complete calendar, but the ensemble of gates, mosques, and minarets creates a distributed astronomical instrument readable by anyone with local knowledge.

### The Sarcophagus as Tuning Box
Pre-literate cultures could standardize musical pitch by carving stone boxes of precise dimensions. The resonant frequency is determined by geometry and material density. Anyone can verify the pitch by humming into the box. **Physical reference standards for sound, analogous to cubit rods for length.**
